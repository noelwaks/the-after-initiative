About Us

Who We Are

The After Initiative is an NGO committed to empowering vulnerable women through vocational skill development. We believe every woman deserves the opportunity to rebuild, grow, and thrive independently. By providing practical training, support, and guidance, we help women gain confidence, achieve financial stability, and create a brighter future for themselves and their communities. Proving that a better after is surely possible.

What We Do

🧵
Sewing & Tailoring Training

We teach women sewing and tailoring skills that can lead to self-employment, creativity, and financial stability.

🧼
Soap Making Training

We provide practical training in soap production, equipping women with a skill that can generate sustainable income.

🌱
Empowerment & Growth

Beyond vocational training, we encourage confidence, independence, and personal development.

🤝
Supportive Community

We create a safe and encouraging environment where women can learn, grow, and rebuild their future.
